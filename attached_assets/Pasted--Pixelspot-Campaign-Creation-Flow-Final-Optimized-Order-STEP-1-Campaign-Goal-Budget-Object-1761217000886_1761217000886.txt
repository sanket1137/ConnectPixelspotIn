🎯 Pixelspot Campaign Creation Flow (Final — Optimized Order)
STEP 1: Campaign Goal & Budget

Objective: Start with intent and spending comfort.

Fields:

Select goal: Awareness | Store Visit | Product Launch

Enter budget: ₹_______ (with slider or input)

UI Copy Example:

“Tell us your goal and budget — we’ll show what’s possible near you.”

STEP 2: Choose Area

Objective: Anchor campaign to a real location.

Interactions:

Search area / pincode or drop a pin on map

Adjust radius (1–10 km)

System detects available screens in that zone

Once the area is set → show live estimate card:

“In Indiranagar (3 km radius), ₹50,000 can reach ~1.2L people across 6 LED screens.”

STEP 3: Set Duration

Objective: Let user define how long they want the campaign to run — but offer automation.

Fields:

Select duration: Custom | Auto decide for me

If “Auto” → system picks optimal number of days based on screen inventory and budget.

If “Custom” → user selects number of days manually.

UI Copy Example:

“Want to maximize your impact? Let Pixelspot auto-plan your duration — or choose days manually.”

STEP 4: Audience & Location Filters (Optional)

Objective: Give power users more control, but keep it hidden by default.

Expandable filters:

Screen type (mall, corporate park, roadside, transit)

Time preference (rush hour / all-day)

Audience density (high-footfall / balanced)

Audience type (office, residential, shopping area)

Design Tip:
Keep this collapsed — most users skip this step.

STEP 5: Smart Plan Suggestions

Objective: Present two clean options.

Smart Plan (Auto-optimized)

Example: “6 screens × 7 days”

Based on their budget, area, and auto duration.

CTA: “Looks Good → Continue”

Customize Plan

“Add/remove screens or adjust days manually.”

Opens detailed view with checkboxes or map pins.

Copy Example:

“With your ₹50,000, you can either spread your reach for a week or go big for 2 days on premium screens.”

STEP 6: Upload Creative & Review

Objective: Finalize and request campaign.

Sections:

Upload creative (video/image)

Auto-validate size + format

Show mockup preview on a sample screen

Then show Summary Card:

🎯 Campaign Goal

📍 Area + Radius

📅 Duration

💰 Budget

👁️ Estimated Reach

🖼 Creative Preview

✅ Primary CTA: “Request Campaign”

Secondary CTA: “Save as Draft”
